System Workflow & Billing Report

Official Implementation & Operational Framework

This report outlines how the MEDFINANCE360 system functions, specifically focusing on the integration with the National EHR and the step-by-step flow of billing from registration to settlement.

1. System Philosophy

MEDFINANCE360 acts as the Financial and Routing Layer of the hospital. While clinical notes may be stored in the National EHR, MEDFINANCE360 manages:

  • Identity Linkage: Capturing NRC and Man Numbers via the External EHR Link.
  • Queue Management: Moving patients between departments.
  • Revenue Control: Real-time charge capturing and centralized payment.

2. The Billing Logic

The system uses a Decentralized Capture / Centralized Settlement model.

2.1 Decentralized Capture

Instead of all billing happening at one desk, charges are "raised" at the point of service. This ensures no service is provided without a digital record of the cost being generated immediately.

  • A Lab Technician raises a Lab Bill.
  • A Pharmacist raises a Pharmacy Bill.
  • A Doctor raises an OPD/Consultation Bill.

2.2 Centralized Settlement

All charges generated across the hospital flow into a single "Pending Cashier Queue." The patient only visits one window (the Cashier) to pay for everything they received during their visit.

3. Step-by-Step Patient Flow

01 Registration Records Clerk Action: Clerk captures only the essential "Identity Link" fields (NRC, Man Number, Names). The system may automatically raise a non-refundable Registration Fee.
02 Authorization Medical Officer Action: Reviews the patient's eligibility and "authorizes" them to proceed. Sets queue status to pending_triage.
03 Triage Nursing Action: Capture BP, Weight, Temperature, and other Vital Signs. Patient moves to waiting_doctor.
04 Consultation Doctor Action: Clinician sees the patient and selects services (Lab, Radiology, Consultation). Selecting a service creates a Pending Bill record instantly.
05 Consolidation Service Point Action: Lab or Pharmacy add additional specific charges (Reagents, Drugs) to the profile. Total Balance increases in real-time.
06 Payment Cashier Action: Pulls up the patient's ID. All pending charges are consolidated. Patient pays full amount (Cash/Mobile Money) or records a Claim (Scheme/Corporate).
07 Fulfillment Service Point Release: Once "Paid" status is detected, Lab results are released or Medications are dispensed.
08 Discharge Final Action: Visit is closed (marked discharged). Archives history and clears patient from active queues.

4. Reporting & Auditing

Data from this flow is reconciled via the Reports & Analytics module:

  • Line Listing: A log of exactly who was registered and their registration status.
  • Department Aggregates: A summary of revenue raised by Lab vs Pharmacy vs Theatre.
  • Cashier Reconciliation: Total collections verified against the generated bills for audit consistency.
System Safeguard: This flow ensures that the Accounts Department can track exactly where revenue is being generated in real-time and prevents "leakage" by ensuring no patient reaches the final fulfillment stage without the system's digital confirmation.
